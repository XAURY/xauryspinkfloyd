The Piper at the Gates of Dawn

Gear: The Piper at the Gates of Dawn

David Gilmour joined the band after this album was made, so this page covers the original four-piece led by Syd Barrett. It still matters for Gilmour's story: he later used the same Binson Echorec delay that Barrett played through here.

Guitars

Barrett's main guitar was a 1962 Fender Esquire, a one-pickup relative of the Telecaster. He decorated the body with small round mirrors, so it is often called the Mirror Disc Telecaster. He used it almost exclusively on the band's early singles and on this album.

He also played slide guitar, at times with a Zippo lighter. The Esquire later disappeared, and today nobody can say for sure where it is.

Effects

The key piece was the Binson Echorec, an Italian echo unit that makes its delay with a spinning magnetic disc and several playback heads. Barrett played through the smaller Baby Echorec, and Richard Wright used an Echorec too. The result is the dark, bubbling echo that runs through the album.

Some writers say Barrett also used a Selmer Buzz Tone fuzz pedal. That is a common belief rather than a confirmed fact, so treat it with care.

Amplifiers

Barrett played through Selmer amplifiers. Sources name a Selmer Treble 'n' Bass amp for the album sessions, and 1967 film footage shows him with a Selmer Stereomaster head, with the Echorec sitting on top of it.

The rest of the band

Richard Wright: the gear list on Richard Wright's official website (compiled by the fan 'aZiMuTh', and not verified in detail) names a Farfisa Combo Compact organ and an acoustic piano for this album, plus a celeste used on this album only. He also ran an Echorec, as mentioned above.

Wikipedia's credits for the song "Flaming" add more instruments played by Wright, including a Hammond organ, a Lowrey organ and a tack piano. These come from Wikipedia and are not on that list.

Roger Waters played bass guitar, and bowed bass on a few tracks. Nick Mason played drums, plus percussion such as timpani and maracas on some songs.

We haven't found a reliable source for the exact model of Waters's bass or Mason's drum kit on this album, so they are left out for now.
