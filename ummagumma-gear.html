Ummagumma

Gear: Ummagumma

Half live, half solo studio pieces. Gilmour kept the same core setup as on the previous album, and used his solo piece to try out some new ideas.

Guitars and amps

Gilmour used his white Stratocaster and natural-finish Telecaster with Selmer amps for both the live and studio recordings. No source says exactly which amps were used for the studio sessions. They came before his Hiwatts, so he probably used his Selmers or amps already in the studio.

The equipment photo on the back cover shows Hiwatt amps that appear to be the ones Gilmour bought in summer 1969.

Gilmour's solo piece

On "The Narrow Way" he played acoustic and electric guitars, bass, keyboards and drums himself. According to Gilmourish, it was the first time he tried a Leslie rotating speaker cabinet, and he also layered guitars and used backwards tape effects.

Effects

A Dallas Arbiter Fuzz Face, a wah-wah and a Binson Echorec delay.

The rest of the band

Richard Wright: the gear list on Richard Wright's official website (compiled by the fan 'aZiMuTh', and not verified in detail) names a Farfisa Combo Compact-Duo organ, a Hammond M-102 'Spinet' organ, an acoustic grand piano and, on this album only, a Mellotron M400 Mark II. Wikipedia credits him with organ, piano, Mellotron and percussion on "Sysyphus".

Roger Waters played acoustic guitars on "Grantchester Meadows" and bass on the live disc. Nick Mason played drums on the live disc and percussion and tape effects on his solo piece, where his wife Lindy plays flute.

We haven't found a reliable source for the exact model of Waters's bass or Mason's drum kit, so they are left out for now.
